Why Isn't My AC Turning Off or Cooling Properly?

Experiencing an air conditioner that runs non-stop or fails to cool your home efficiently can be frustrating, especially during the hot Texas summers. Let's explore some common reasons why this might happen and when it's time to call in a professional from Austin for assistance.

Common Reasons Your AC Won't Shut Off

Thermostat Issues

A malfunctioning thermostat can prevent your AC from turning off even when the desired temperature is reached. Ensure your thermostat is set correctly and functioning properly. Consider replacing the batteries or recalibrating it if necessary.

Dirty Air Filters

Clogged air filters can restrict airflow, causing your system to work harder and longer. Replace or clean your filters every 1-3 months to maintain efficiency.

Frozen Evaporator Coils

When evaporator coils freeze, it can prevent your AC from shutting off. This typically happens due to low refrigerant levels or reduced airflow. Check for visible signs of ice and allow the coils to thaw if necessary.

Why Isn't My AC Cooling Properly?

Low Refrigerant Levels

If your AC is blowing warm air or not cooling as expected, it might be due to low refrigerant levels. This often indicates a leak, which should be addressed by a professional.

Blocked or Dirty Condenser Unit

The outdoor condenser unit can become blocked with dirt, leaves, or debris, affecting its ability to cool effectively. Regularly clean the area around the unit to ensure proper airflow.

Inadequate Insulation

Poor insulation can lead to uneven cooling and your AC working overtime. Check your home’s insulation, particularly in the attic and windows, to improve efficiency.

When to Call an Austin HVAC Pro

While some issues can be resolved with a bit of DIY effort, others require professional intervention. If your system still struggles after basic troubleshooting or you suspect issues like refrigerant leaks, it's time to contact an expert.
