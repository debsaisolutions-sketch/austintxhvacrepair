How Often Should You Change Your Air Filters in Austin's Climate?

Changing your HVAC air filters regularly is crucial, especially in Austin, where the climate can vary significantly throughout the year. A clean filter ensures your system runs efficiently, improves indoor air quality, and can even extend the lifespan of your HVAC unit.

Understanding Austin's Climate and Its Impact on Air Filters

Austin's humid subtropical climate means air conditioners work hard during the long, hot summers. With pollen and dust levels often high, filters can become clogged faster than in other areas. This makes regular maintenance an essential task for homeowners.

Factors Affecting How Frequently You Should Change Your Filter

  • Type of Filter: Basic fiberglass filters may require more frequent changes, while pleated filters can last longer.
  • Household Size: More occupants can lead to quicker accumulation of dust and debris.
  • Pets: Homes with pets should consider changing filters more often due to pet dander.
  • Allergies: If household members suffer from allergies, frequent filter changes can help reduce symptoms.

Recommended Schedule for Changing Your Air Filters

In general, it's recommended that you change your air filters every 1-3 months. However, during peak summer months, you might need to do this monthly to ensure optimal performance.

How to Change Your Air Filters

  1. Turn off your HVAC system to ensure safety.
  2. Locate the air filter compartment, typically near the air handler or return vent.
  3. Remove the old filter and dispose of it properly.
  4. Insert the new filter, ensuring the arrows on the filter frame point in the direction of airflow.
  5. Secure the compartment and turn your system back on.

If you're unsure about the process, consider consulting a local HVAC professional for guidance. Regular maintenance, including filter changes, can prevent more significant issues and keep your system running smoothly.
