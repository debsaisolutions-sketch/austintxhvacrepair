Is Your A/C Making Strange Noises? Here's When to Worry

Hearing strange noises from your A/C unit can be unsettling, especially during the sweltering Texas summers. Knowing when to worry and call a professional can save you time and money. Here’s a guide to help you identify common A/C issues based on the sounds you might hear.

Common A/C Noises and Their Causes

Banging or Clanking

If you hear banging or clanking coming from your A/C, it could indicate a loose or broken part inside the compressor. This is a sign that the unit needs immediate attention from a professional, as prolonged operation could cause further damage.

Buzzing

A persistent buzzing noise may suggest electrical issues, such as faulty wiring or a malfunctioning fan motor. This type of problem can be challenging to diagnose and repair without the proper expertise.

Squealing or Screeching

Squealing sounds are often related to belt problems or motor bearings that need lubrication. While this might not require urgent repair, it's wise to address these issues before they escalate.

When to Call a Professional

While some minor noises might not be cause for immediate concern, others, such as banging or buzzing, usually indicate more severe issues. In the Texas Hill Country, where your A/C is a lifeline against the heat, it's crucial to seek professional help promptly to avoid costly repairs or replacements.

  • Banging or clanking: Call a pro immediately.
  • Buzzing: Schedule an inspection as soon as possible.
  • Squealing: Address at your earliest convenience.
